# MVP Scope

## Primary Domain
Medical Devices

## Primary Data Scope
- MAUDE / MDR public event data
- MedWatch reporting guidance
- eMDR workflow guidance

## Time Range
2015-present

## Primary User Workflows
1. Safety intelligence and trend analysis
2. Reporting guidance and reporting support

## Questions the MVP Should Support
- Which device problem types have increased or decreased over time?
- How do reports vary by manufacturer, device category, or year?
- Which event types are linked to more serious outcomes?
- What does a specific reporting field or reporting concept mean?
- What source information is likely needed for a specific report section?
- What obvious gaps or inconsistencies appear in a draft reporting workflow?

## Out of Scope for MVP
- autonomous report submission
- device-level causal adjudication
- patient-level risk prediction
- legal or regulatory sign-off
- full production-grade workflow integration
