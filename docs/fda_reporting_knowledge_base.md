# FDA Reporting Knowledge Base (Week 5 Seed)

## Medical Device Reporting
Medical Device Reporting (MDR) is one of the FDA's postmarket surveillance tools for monitoring device performance and detecting potential safety issues.

## Core Workflow Concepts
- Mandatory reporters include manufacturers, importers, and device user facilities.
- Voluntary reporters may include health care professionals, patients, caregivers, and consumers.
- MedWatch forms are used for safety reporting workflows.
- Form 3500 is used for voluntary reporting.
- Form 3500A is used for mandatory reporting when required.
- Electronic Medical Device Reporting (eMDR) supports electronic submission workflows for mandatory reporters.

## Reporting Copilot Goals
The reporting copilot is intended to:
- explain what a reporting field or concept means
- suggest likely source information needed for a field or section
- distinguish voluntary and mandatory reporting contexts
- explain workflow concepts, not submit reports automatically

## Important Limitations
- Public FDA guidance explains reporting expectations, but actual report values must come from the reporter's records.
- The copilot is not a substitute for regulatory, legal, or quality review.
- Reporting responsibilities differ by reporter type and workflow.
